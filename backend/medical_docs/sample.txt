Typhoid fever is a bacterial infection caused by Salmonella typhi.
Symptoms include prolonged fever, fatigue, headache, nausea, abdominal pain.
Treatment includes antibiotics such as ciprofloxacin or azithromycin.
Patients should drink plenty of fluids and rest.

Diabetes is a chronic condition where blood sugar levels are too high.
Type 1 diabetes is an autoimmune condition. Type 2 is lifestyle related.
Symptoms include frequent urination, excessive thirst, blurred vision.
Treatment includes insulin, metformin, and lifestyle changes.

Pneumonia is an infection that inflames the air sacs in the lungs.
Symptoms include cough, fever, chills, and difficulty breathing.
Treatment includes antibiotics, rest, and fluids.
